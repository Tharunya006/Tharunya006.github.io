About Me

Curious, creative and eager to build

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology at Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal. My interest in technology grew out of a strong foundation in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Computer Science during my pre-university studies in Mangalore.

I enjoy working across the stack: designing clean front-end interfaces with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and connecting them to back-end logic and relational databases.
